Langar Bar Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

This policy explains how Langar Bar in Dugo Selo, Croatia processes information when you use the Langar Bar website or mobile application, Langar Club, online ordering, reservations, rewards, notifications and related customer services.

Information we process

Depending on the features you use, we may process your name, email address, telephone number, birthday, account identifiers, app language, order and reservation details, delivery address, loyalty/reward activity, referral activity, feedback and reviews, customer-service messages, notification preferences, device/push identifiers and basic technical logs needed to operate and secure the service.

Why we use the information

We use this information to create and protect your account, process orders and reservations, provide Langar Club rewards and referrals, communicate order status and estimated preparation time, send notifications you have enabled, answer support questions, prevent fraud and duplicate reward abuse, improve the service and meet legal, tax, accounting and security obligations.

Service providers

Langar Bar uses contracted technology providers, including Supabase for cloud database/authentication infrastructure and OneSignal for push notifications. These providers process information only as needed to provide their contracted services and subject to their applicable security and data-protection terms.

Sale and advertising

Langar Bar does not sell your personal data. We do not use your Langar Club personal information for third-party behavioural advertising.

Retention

Account and customer-service data is retained only as long as necessary for the purposes described above. Certain transaction, fiscal, accounting, fraud-prevention or security records may need to be retained for the period required by Croatian or EU law. When an account is deleted, records that must legally remain are anonymized where reasonably possible.

Your choices and rights

You may update account information, change notification preferences and request access, correction or deletion of personal data. Where applicable under EU data-protection law, you may also have rights to restriction, objection and data portability.

Account deletion

Langar Club users can delete their account from the mobile app under More → Delete my account. If you no longer have the app, use our public deletion page: Delete Langar Bar Account.

Notifications

Push notifications require device permission and may be disabled in the app/device settings. Transactional inbox messages related to orders or reservations may still be shown inside the app where required to provide the service.

Children

Langar Club is not intended to knowingly collect accounts from children without appropriate consent. Alcohol-related content and sales are subject to applicable age restrictions; Langar Bar does not sell or serve alcohol to persons under 18.
