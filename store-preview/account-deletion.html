Delete your Langar Bar account

Langar Bar · Dugo Selo, Croatia

If you created a Langar Club account, you can permanently delete it from the mobile app under More → Delete my account.

Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have access to the app, send an email from the email address registered to your Langar Club account to langaarbar@gmail.com with the subject Langar Bar account deletion.

Please include the name and phone number used on the account so we can verify the request. We may ask for limited verification before completing deletion to prevent unauthorized account removal.

What is deleted

Your Langar Club authentication account, profile, contact details, loyalty/reward data, notification identifiers, reservations, inbox messages and other account-linked customer data are deleted.

What may be retained

Records that must be retained for legal, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention or security reasons may remain for the required retention period. Where possible, retained operational or transaction records are anonymized so they are no longer linked to your Langar Club identity.

Deletion requests are normally processed as soon as verification is complete and no later than 30 days unless a legal retention obligation applies.
