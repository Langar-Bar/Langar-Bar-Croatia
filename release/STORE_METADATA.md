## Full description (EN)
Langar Bar brings ordering, reservations and Langar Club rewards into one simple app.

Browse the current menu and prices, choose dine-in, pick-up or delivery when available, customize your order and see your running order total before checkout. After your order is accepted, follow the estimated preparation time and order status directly in the app.

With Langar Club you can create a customer profile, receive eligible welcome and birthday rewards, collect Langar Credit, use digital reward cards and invite friends with your referral code.

You can also reserve a table, send feedback, view events and special experiences, submit a sushi pre-order when available, ask coffee questions and learn more through Coffee Academy content.

Staff members use the same installed app through a secure Staff/Admin login. Administrative access is protected by account role and is not available to ordinary customer accounts.

Key features:
• Current menu and prices
• Dine-in, pick-up and delivery ordering when enabled
• Live order status and estimated ready time
• Table reservations
• Langar Club profile and digital QR
• Rewards, Langar Credit and referral benefits
• Inbox and push notifications
• Events, feedback and coffee experience features
• Secure staff/admin console for authorized Langar Bar staff

Some services are available only during opening hours or when enabled by Langar Bar. Alcohol-related products, if offered, are subject to applicable Croatian age restrictions.

## Review notes
The app is primarily a customer ordering and loyalty app. Staff/Admin functionality exists inside the same binary but requires a valid active admin account in Supabase `admin_members`. Ordinary users cannot access administrative data.

A reviewer can browse Home, Menu, Order and More without a Langar Club account. Account creation is required only for account-specific functions such as Langar Club profile/rewards and some personalized services.

Account deletion is available inside the native app under More → Delete my account. Public deletion instructions are available at the account-deletion URL above.
